How to export cookies.txt

  1. Sign in to youtube.com in a normal browser window.
  2. Install a cookies exporter extension, or run this in that browser’s developer console on youtube.com and copy the output:

    document.cookie will not work — YouTube cookies are HttpOnly. Use a cookies-export extension, that is the reliable route.
  3. Open the exported cookies.txt here and upload it.

Security note: these cookies are a live login to your YouTube account. Anyone with access to the server can read them. Upload it only to your own server, and sign out of YouTube everywhere afterwards to invalidate them.
