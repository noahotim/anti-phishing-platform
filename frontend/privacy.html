BOTIMPHISHGUARD privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026. This policy covers the BOTIMPHISHGUARD browser extension and the phishguard-8vri.onrender.com server it connects to.

What the extension collects

BOTIMPHISHGUARD checks the address of each page you open against the guard server so it can warn you about phishing, malware, gambling and other harmful sites. To do that it sends the following to the server:

  • the web address (URL) of the page you are trying to open
  • the website domain, so a block can be recorded in the live activity feed
  • the result of the check (for example: blocked, suspicious, or safe)

It does not read, store or send the contents of the pages you visit, your form input, your passwords, your browsing history in full, or your personal files. It does not use cookies for advertising and it contains no third-party tracking code.

What is stored on the server

  • Live activity records: domain, time, and why something was blocked or flagged
  • Your account details: email address and a hashed password, if you created an account
  • Sites you add to your own trusted or whitelist list
  • Feedback messages you send

YouTube video addresses you choose to download are used only to fetch that file. They are not stored after the file is sent to you.

Who can see your data

Only the owner and administrators of this BOTIMPHISHGUARD server. There is no third-party analytics, advertising or data sharing.

Advertising and affiliate links

The extension contains no ads and no affiliate links. It blocks them on pages you visit.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no information from them deliberately.

Your choices

You can turn the guard off for one site by continuing once, permanently allow a site with “Add to whitelist”, or uninstall the extension at any time from your browser’s extension page. Uninstalling stops all data being sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the new version is published at https://phishguard-8vri.onrender.com/app/privacy.html.
