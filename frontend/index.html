Did you know

  • One character that looks identical can be a fake domain — e.g. paypa1.com or еxample.com (Cyrillic “е”).
  • A trusted name as a subdomain, like example.com.secure-login.malware.io, is a classic trick.
  • Check the actual address in your browser, not the friendly text of a link.
  • When in doubt, don’t click. Report it below and let the team verify.
